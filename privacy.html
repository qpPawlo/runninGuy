runBOB — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Telegram bot @runBOB_bot and the runBOB Mini App.

What we store

  • Game progress (level, coins, upgrades, unlocked skins, settings) — on your device and in your own Telegram Cloud Storage, so it follows your Telegram account.
  • Leaderboard entry — your Telegram first name (and the first letter of your last name, if set), an internal player ID derived from your Telegram ID, and the number of coins you earned in the game. This entry is visible to other players in the leaderboard.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, messages, location, payment details or any other personal data. There are no ads and no third-party analytics in the game.

Bot messages

The bot only answers the commands you send it (/start, /top, /help) and inline requests (@runBOB_bot). Messages are processed to generate a reply and are not stored.

Removing your data

You can reset your progress at any time in the game: Settings → Reset progress. To remove your leaderboard entry, message the bot owner via Telegram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
